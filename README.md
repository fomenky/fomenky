## Proof of work

Real AI solutions, each with a live, browser-readable report — no setup required.

### Financial Intelligence Assistant
The problem: brokers drown in overnight news, prices, and filings before markets open.
The outcome: a grounded, source-cited Q&A assistant — answer quality improved 0.41 to 0.76 through systematic optimization, reducing compliance risk from unsupported answers.
[View report](https://fomenky.github.io/financial-intelligence-rag/) · [Repo](https://github.com/fomenky/financial-intelligence-rag)

### Delivery Exception Automation (Multi-Agent)
The problem: failed deliveries triaged by hand — slow, inconsistent, costly.
The outcome: an AI agent team that resolves, escalates, and notifies automatically with privacy enforced by design — 100% accuracy on triage, escalation, and tooling across the benchmark.
[View report](https://fomenky.github.io/delivery-exception-multi-agent-system/) · [Repo](https://github.com/fomenky/delivery-exception-multi-agent-system)

### Hotel Policy Assistant (Deployable App)
The problem: staff spend 8–12 minutes per complex guest query, with a 15–20% error rate.
The outcome: a deployable assistant that answers from policy with cited sources and routes only low-confidence cases to a human — shipped as a containerized app, not a demo.
[View report](https://fomenky.github.io/hotel-qna-rag-app/) · [Repo](https://github.com/fomenky/hotel-qna-rag-app)

### Predictive Maintenance for Wind Turbines
The problem: unplanned drivetrain failures cost 7–21 days of lost revenue each.
The outcome: an ML model that flags faults early from sensor data, tuned to catch failures before they cascade into costly damage across a 1,150-turbine fleet scenario.
[View report](https://fomenky.github.io/wind-turbine-fault-detection/) · [Repo](https://github.com/fomenky/wind-turbine-fault-detection)
